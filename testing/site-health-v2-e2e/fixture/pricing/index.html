Pricing

Fixture Co offers simple plans that scale with the number of fixture sites you need to crawl. Every plan includes unlimited deterministic crawls, full page type classification, and exportable reports. Choose the plan that fits your team and upgrade or downgrade at any time without losing history.

Starter plan

The Starter plan covers small teams that need a handful of monitored sites, weekly crawls, and the core site health dashboard with per page type breakdowns and issue catalogs.

Growth plan

The Growth plan adds daily crawls, larger inventories, monitored URL sets, and priority processing for bigger sites with thousands of pages across many sections and locales.

All plans are billed monthly and include a fourteen day evaluation window with full access to every feature. Nonprofit and education discounts are available on request through the contact page.
