About Fixture Co

Fixture Co was founded to make crawler testing boring. We believe the best fixture is the one nobody has to think about: stable markup, stable content, and stable responses, release after release.

The team is distributed across three time zones and united by a love of deterministic pipelines. We maintain the fixture catalog, document every signal each page exercises, and keep the site fast enough for the politest crawlers.
