Fixture Co — deterministic fixtures for crawler testing

Fixture Co publishes small, stable websites that exercising crawlers can discover, fetch, and analyze. Every page on this site exists so that a site health crawler can classify it into exactly one page type. This homepage is intentionally link heavy and light on prose, the way real homepages are.

Our fixtures cover blogs, pricing pages, documentation, FAQs, product pages, category listings, about pages, and deliberately ambiguous pages that carry no classification signal at all. Crawlers can follow the navigation above to reach every corner of the site in one hop from the root.

The content here is static HTML rendered on the server. There is no JavaScript gating, no click to expand sections, and no lazy loading, so the raw response body always contains the full visible text of the page.
